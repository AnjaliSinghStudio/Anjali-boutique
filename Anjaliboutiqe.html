About Us

Learn more about our boutique and tailoring services

Welcome to Anjali Boutique and Tailoring Materials

Your trusted destination for quality tailoring materials and professional boutique services in Aya Nagar, New Delhi.

At Anjali Boutique and Tailoring Materials, we take pride in offering a wide range of high-quality dress materials, tailoring supplies, and expert stitching services. Our shop is located in the heart of Aya Nagar, Phase 5, conveniently situated near Deva Departmental Store.

We specialize in ladies suit stitching, blouse stitching, boutique stitching, and custom tailoring services. Our commitment to quality, reasonable prices, and exceptional customer service has made us a trusted name in the community.
